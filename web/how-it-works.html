Guide

How it works

1. Plan chuno

Catalog mein category kholo, plan ka description, price aur stock dekho.

2. Wallet bharo

Khareedari wallet balance (USD) se hoti hai. Pehle top-up karo, tarike yahan hain.

3. Quantity aur confirm

Product page par quantity chuno. Confirm screen par total price aur coupon discount (agar hai) dikhta hai.

4. Delivery

  • Auto plans: account details turant chat mein aate hain. Bahut items hon to PDF file milti hai.
  • Manual plans: order "processing" mein jaata hai aur admin team deliver karti hai; delivery par aapko message aata hai.

Pre-order

Agar koi auto plan sold out hai, to aap abhi pay karke pre-order kar sakte ho. Stock aate hi items apne aap deliver ho jaate hain, aam taur par kuch hi ghanton mein.

Order status

pending, processing, completed, cancelled, refunded.
