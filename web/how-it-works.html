Guide

How it works

1. Choose a plan

Open a category and review the description, price and stock of each plan.

2. Add funds

Purchases are paid from your wallet balance (USD). Top up first; the methods are listed here.

3. Select quantity and confirm

Pick a quantity on the product page. The confirmation screen shows the total and any coupon discount.

4. Delivery

  • Automatic plans: account details appear instantly. Large orders are provided as a PDF file.
  • Manual plans: the order moves to "processing" and our team delivers it; you are notified when it is done.

Pre-order

If an automatic plan is sold out, you can pay now and pre-order it. It is delivered automatically when stock arrives, usually within a few hours.

Order status

pending, processing, completed, cancelled, refunded.
