Wallet & top-up

All purchases are paid from your wallet balance (USD). To add funds, sign in and open the Wallet & top-up tab.

Open wallet

Top-up methods

  • UPI: choose an amount, pay by UPI and submit the 12-digit UTR. Verified automatically when payment alerts are enabled, otherwise approved by an admin.
  • Binance ID: transfer to the Binance ID shown and submit the transaction or order ID. Verified automatically when enabled, otherwise approved by an admin.
  • USDT (BEP20): send the exact amount shown to the address and submit the transaction hash. It is verified on the blockchain automatically.

We never ask for card details or passwords on this website. A UTR or transaction hash can only be used once.
