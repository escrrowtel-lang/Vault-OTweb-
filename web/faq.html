FAQ

Kya prices USD mein hain?

Haan, saari prices USD ($) mein hain. UPI se top-up karne par INR mein pay hota hai.

Kharidne ka tarika kya hai?

Plan chuno aur wallet balance se pay karo. Wallet top-up ke tarike Wallet page par hain.

Delivery kitne time mein hoti hai?

Auto plans turant. Manual plans admin team deliver karti hai. Pre-order stock aate hi, aam taur par kuch hi ghanton mein.

Pre-order kya hota hai?

Sold out auto plan ko aap abhi pay karke book kar sakte ho; stock aate hi apne aap deliver hota hai.

Coupon kaise kaam karta hai?

Coupon percentage discount deta hai aur ek hi baar use hota hai. Confirm screen par discount dikhta hai.

Refund milta hai?

Rules Refund policy page par hain. Confirm karne se pehle bhi dikhte hain.

Referral reward kitna hai?

Shuruaati reward $0.10 hai jab aapka bulaya hua user pehla deposit kare. Ye badal sakta hai.
