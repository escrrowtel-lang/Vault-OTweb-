Frequently asked questions

Are prices in US dollars?

Yes. All prices are shown in USD ($). If you top up with UPI, you pay the equivalent amount in INR.

How do I buy a plan?

Choose a plan and pay from your wallet balance. The Wallet page explains how to add funds.

How long does delivery take?

Auto-delivery plans are delivered instantly. Manual plans are delivered by our team. Pre-orders are delivered as soon as stock arrives, usually within a few hours.

What is a pre-order?

If an auto-delivery plan is sold out, you can pay now and reserve it. It is delivered automatically as soon as new stock is added.

How do coupons work?

A coupon gives a percentage discount and can be used once. The discount is shown on the confirmation screen.

Can I get a refund?

Our rules are on the Refund policy page and are shown again before you confirm an order.

How much is the referral reward?

The reward is currently $0.10, paid when someone you invite makes their first deposit. It may change over time.
